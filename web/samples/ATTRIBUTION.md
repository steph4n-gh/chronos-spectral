# Sample Video Attribution

All five real-camera calibration and demonstration clips in `web/samples/` are transcoded to `640x360` progressive H.264 (`yuv420p`, `+faststart`) from open-licensed Wikimedia Commons footage:

- `juggling_ballistic.mp4` (`60 fps`, Mode A Ballistic Free-Flight): Cropped to the height-ruled chalkboard free-flight region from `File:Zeitsymmetrie (jonglieren).webm` by Rhetos / Mathe-AC Lernwerkstatt Aachen (`https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Zeitsymmetrie_(jonglieren).webm`), licensed under [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). Under ShareAlike terms, this cropped video adaptation remains licensed under CC BY-SA 4.0.
- `roundabout_occlusion.mp4` (`24 fps`, Mode A Short-Gap Hermite Occlusion Bridge & Multi-Vehicle Crossing): Cropped onto the northern approach and overhead highway sign gantry from `File:Roundabout 14 61.webm` by Wikideas1 (`https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Roundabout_14_61.webm`), dedicated to the public domain under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).
- `harmonic_metronome_phase.mp4` (`30 fps`, Mode B Analytic Hilbert Quadrature & Eulerian Contrast): Cropped and scaled from `File:Metronome.webm` by Daniel Eriksson / Belteshassar (`https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Metronome.webm`), licensed under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
