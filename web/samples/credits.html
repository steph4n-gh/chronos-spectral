Semi-Synthetic Physical Metrology, BOS, and Decoy Fixtures (8.0 s to 9.0 s)

  • bos_thermal_plume.mp4 (9.0 s, 25 fps)
    Semi-synthetic controlled tripod Background-Oriented Schlieren (BOS) composition (not raw laboratory Schlieren film) built on real natural gravel photography (File:Gray Gravel Rock Texture by Photos Public Domain.jpg, dedicated under CC0 1.0). Frames 0..15 hold an unperturbed 16-frame static reference, followed by paraxial Gladstone-Dale refractive ray deflection from a 2D Boussinesq Navier-Stokes buoyancy plume (peak shift 1.38 px, subtle sensor noise).
  • perspective_flyby_decoy.mp4 (9.0 s, 25 fps)
    Semi-synthetic pinhole-projected constant-3D-velocity rectilinear approach (0.0 s to 5.4 s, zero 3D acceleration with nonzero 2D perspective acceleration) and smooth horizontal camera pan (5.4 s to 9.0 s) over a textured airfield horizon using File:Grey speckled clean asphalt seamless texture.jpg by Sisters.seamless (CC0 1.0) and File:Stone church wall texture limerick ireland.jpg by Gaz Davidson (Public Domain).
  • bos_handheld_failure.mp4 (8.0 s, 25 fps)
    Semi-synthetic tripod-shock and handheld-sway BOS failure composition sharing the CC0 1.0 gravel backdrop and Gladstone-Dale plume (steady tripod 0.00 s to 2.56 s, tripod shock 2.56 s to 3.36 s, handheld sway with roll/scale breathing and exposure blur 3.36 s to 5.96 s, displaced post-shock rest 5.96 s to 8.00 s).
